# Fundamentals to AI

This course gives a broad introduction to the fundamentals of AI, including intelligent Agents, searching algorythms and ethical considerations

---

## What we covered

- Analyze the characteristics of intelligent systems and different applications of AI
- Compare different research areas covered by Artificial Intelligence.
- Compare agents, environment, and types of AI agents
- Apply search strategies and local search algorithms in solving problems
- Recognize and address real-world problems in the field of AI using search.

Focus: Critical evaluation over rote memorization
